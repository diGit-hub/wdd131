Spirit Types

Characters are classified by their spirit type:

  • Power: Focus on raw damage and strength
  • Agility: Speed and assassination techniques
  • Support: Buff allies and provide healing
  • Control: Manipulate the battlefield

The Seven Shrek Devils

The main characters form a team called the Seven Shrek Devils at Shrek Academy. They participate in competitions and become legends in the spirit master world.

Members include Tang San, Xiao Wu, Dai Mubai, Oscar, Ning Rongrong, Ma Hongjun, and Zhu Zhuqing.
