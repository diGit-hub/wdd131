This site provides information about the world of Douluo Dalu, including the spirit rank system and key characters from the series.

What is Douluo Dalu?

Douluo Dalu is a Chinese web novel and manhua series set in a world where people can awaken spirits at age six. Those with spirit power become spirit masters by hunting spirit beasts and absorbing spirit rings to gain abilities.

The story follows Tang San, a young man who reincarnates into this world with knowledge of hidden weapons and poisons from his previous life.

Spirit Power System

There are ten ranks of spirit masters, each requiring a new spirit ring:

  • Spirit Scholar (Lv. 1-10): Beginning stage
  • Spirit Master (Lv. 11-20): First ring acquired
  • Grand Spirit Master (Lv. 21-30): Second ring
  • Spirit Elder (Lv. 31-40): Third ring
  • Spirit Ancestor (Lv. 41-50): Fourth ring
  • Spirit King (Lv. 51-60): Fifth ring
  • Spirit Emperor (Lv. 61-70): Sixth ring
  • Spirit Sage (Lv. 71-80): Seventh ring
  • Spirit Douluo (Lv. 81-90): Eighth ring
  • Title Douluo (Lv. 91-99): Ninth ring, peak of humanity
