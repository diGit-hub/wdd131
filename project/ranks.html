About Spirit Rings

Each rank requires absorbing a spirit ring from a defeated spirit beast. The ring color indicates the beast's age:

  • White: 10 years - weakest rings
  • Yellow: 100 years - standard for beginners
  • Purple: 1,000 years - mid-tier power
  • Black: 10,000 years - elite rings
  • Red: 100,000 years - legendary rings

The older the beast, the stronger the ring, but also more dangerous to absorb. Absorbing a ring too powerful can destroy a spirit master's foundation.

Rank Progression

Spirit masters must hunt spirit beasts in order:

  1. First ring: Best at 10+ years, ideally 100 years
  2. Second ring: Best at 100+ years
  3. Third ring: Best at 1,000+ years
  4. Fourth ring: Best at 1,000+ years
  5. Fifth ring: Must be 10,000+ years
  6. Sixth ring: 10,000+ years
  7. Seventh ring: 10,000+ years minimum
  8. Eighth ring: 10,000+ years
  9. Ninth ring: Ideally 100,000 years
